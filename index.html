Langtang Valley Trek

Langtang Region, Nepal

4,400m · Kyanjin Ri
~65 km Trail
22–25 Jan 2026
Langtang, Nepal

Kathmandu → Syafru → Bamboo → Langtang Valley → Gompa → Kyanjin Ri

Risky roads, yet each step carrying a different kind of beauty. Crossing the Jhalunge suspension bridges over Langtang Khola with the best crew, watching Lirung peak get closer with a different kind of magic in every timelapse. Walking through Old Langtang Valley ; the whole settlement destroyed by the massive rock-ice avalanche during the 2015 earthquake, and seeing it today, fully transitioned from "recovery mode," back, beautiful, and better than ever. Langtang, you're worth the struggle.
